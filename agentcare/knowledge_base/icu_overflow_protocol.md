# ICU Overflow and Critical Care Capacity Protocol

**Applies to:** ICU_UTILIZATION incidents, critical care bed shortages.

When ICU utilization exceeds 90%, the following steps apply, in order:

1. **Immediate triage review.** The on-call intensivist reviews all current ICU
   patients for step-down eligibility — patients stable enough to transfer to
   a general ward or high-dependency unit within 2 hours.
2. **Activate surge capacity.** Convert designated post-anesthesia care unit
   (PACU) bays or high-dependency unit beds to temporary ICU-capable beds if
   the hospital's surge staffing ratio allows it.
3. **Divert non-critical elective admissions.** Any elective surgery expected
   to require ICU-level post-op care is postponed until utilization drops
   below 85%.
4. **Notify regional transfer network.** If utilization remains above 95% for
   more than 2 hours after steps 1-3, notify the regional critical-care
   transfer coordinator to identify receiving hospitals for new critical
   admissions.
5. **Staffing escalation.** Call in on-call ICU nursing staff if the
   nurse-to-patient ratio would otherwise exceed 1:2.

Do not discharge a patient solely to free capacity without a documented
clinical step-down assessment — capacity pressure is never sufficient
justification alone.
