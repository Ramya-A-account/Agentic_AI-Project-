# Hospital-Wide Bed Capacity Crisis Protocol

**Applies to:** BED_OCCUPANCY and DEPARTMENT_BED_OCCUPANCY incidents.

Hospital-wide bed occupancy above 90%, or any single department above 95%,
triggers this protocol.

1. **Discharge acceleration review.** The bed management team reviews all
   patients medically cleared for discharge in the past 12 hours and
   prioritizes same-day discharge paperwork and transport arrangements.
2. **Cross-department load balancing.** Patients awaiting a bed in an
   over-capacity department are assessed for suitability to board in an
   adjacent department (e.g. General Medicine overflow into Orthopedics)
   under joint care.
3. **Delay non-urgent elective admissions.** New elective admissions
   scheduled for the next 24 hours are reviewed and, where clinically safe,
   deferred by 24-48 hours.
4. **Escalate to hospital operations manager.** If occupancy remains above
   95% hospital-wide for more than 4 hours, the operations manager convenes
   a capacity huddle with department heads.
5. **Activate overflow space.** Designated overflow areas (day-surgery unit,
   observation unit) are opened for short-stay patients only.

Department-specific overflow (e.g. Cardiology at 97%) should first attempt
load balancing to departments with available capacity before escalating
hospital-wide.
