# Staffing Shortage Escalation Protocol

**Applies to:** Incidents where department staffing falls below safe
nurse-to-patient or physician-to-patient ratios, often co-occurring with
bed occupancy or ICU utilization incidents.

1. **Assess current ratios.** Charge nurse confirms current staff-to-patient
   ratio against the department's minimum safe staffing standard.
2. **Internal redeployment.** Check for available staff in departments
   currently under their capacity threshold who are cross-trained for the
   affected unit.
3. **Call in on-call and per-diem staff.** If internal redeployment is
   insufficient, activate the on-call roster and per-diem staffing pool.
4. **Agency staffing.** If ratios cannot be restored within 2 hours through
   internal means, contact the approved staffing agency for temporary
   coverage.
5. **Temporary admission hold.** As a last resort, and only with manager
   approval, temporarily pause new admissions to the affected department
   until safe staffing ratios are restored.

Patient safety ratios take priority over capacity targets — do not accept
new admissions into a unit that cannot staff them safely.
