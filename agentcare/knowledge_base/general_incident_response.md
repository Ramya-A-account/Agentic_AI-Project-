# General Operational Incident Response Guidelines

**Applies to:** Any operational KPI incident not covered by a more specific
protocol.

1. **Verify the data.** Confirm the triggering value against a secondary
   source before acting, to rule out a data quality or sensor issue.
2. **Assess trend, not just snapshot.** Compare the current value to the
   baseline and recent trend — a single-point spike may not require the same
   response as a sustained upward trend.
3. **Notify the relevant department head.** The department associated with
   the incident should be informed within 15 minutes of detection.
4. **Document the response.** Any manager action taken in response should be
   logged with a brief rationale for the audit trail.
5. **Review at shift handover.** Any incident still open at shift change
   must be explicitly handed over to the incoming manager, not left to be
   discovered independently.

When in doubt about which specific protocol applies, treat patient safety
and continuity of care as the overriding priority over operational
efficiency metrics.
