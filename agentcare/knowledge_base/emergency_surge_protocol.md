# Emergency Department Surge Protocol

**Applies to:** EMERGENCY_EVENTS incidents, Emergency department overload.

Triggered when emergency event volume exceeds 60 events (warning) or 100
events (critical) within the monitoring window.

1. **Activate additional triage lanes.** Open secondary triage stations to
   reduce door-to-triage time below the 10-minute target.
2. **Call in surge staffing.** Page on-call emergency physicians and nurses
   per the facility's surge staffing roster.
3. **Fast-track low-acuity cases.** Patients triaged as low-acuity (ESI 4-5)
   are redirected to the fast-track area or urgent care partner clinic to
   preserve emergency department capacity for higher-acuity patients.
4. **Coordinate with inpatient bed management.** Emergency department
   boarding times are monitored; if patients are boarding more than 4 hours
   awaiting an inpatient bed, escalate jointly with the Bed Capacity Crisis
   Protocol.
5. **Divert notification.** If the department cannot safely accept further
   ambulance traffic, notify regional EMS dispatch of temporary diversion
   status, subject to hospital administrator approval.

Diversion is a last resort and requires administrator sign-off before
activation.
