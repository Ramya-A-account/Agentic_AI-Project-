# Medicine and Inventory Shortage Protocol

**Applies to:** Low-stock alerts on critical medicine or medical supply
inventory.

1. **Confirm actual stock.** Cross-check the system-reported stock level
   against a physical count in the affected department's supply room, as
   discrepancies are common with high-turnover items.
2. **Internal transfer.** Check other departments' stock for surplus of the
   same item that can be transferred immediately.
3. **Emergency reorder.** If hospital-wide stock is genuinely below the
   reorder threshold, place an emergency order with the primary supplier,
   flagged for expedited delivery.
4. **Substitute protocol.** For medicines, consult the hospital formulary
   for an approved clinically-equivalent substitute while the reorder is in
   transit.
5. **Escalate to pharmacy director.** If the shortage affects a
   life-critical medicine (e.g. emergency cardiac drugs, anesthesia agents)
   and cannot be resolved within 4 hours, escalate directly to the pharmacy
   director for cross-hospital-network sourcing.

Life-critical medicine shortages are always treated as high priority
regardless of overall inventory value.
